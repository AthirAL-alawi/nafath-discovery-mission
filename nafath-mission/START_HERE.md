# Start Here

## What this mission is

The Nafath Discovery Mission is the first stage of selection. It shows us how
you research a professional topic, organize information, follow precise
instructions, and use GitHub, the tool used daily by technical teams.

## What you will do

1. **Research** one scenario that combines six IT fields: Software, AI, Mobile,
   Networking, Cybersecurity and Data.
2. **Write** a short professional brief (`brief.md`) with one resource for each field.
3. **Deliver** the brief in your own public GitHub repository, following an exact
   sequence of commits.
4. **Submit** the submission form.

## Time

- Expected effort: **3 to 4 hours**. You do not need to do it in one sitting.
- Deadline: **[Tuesday, DD Month YYYY] at 11:59 PM, Oman time (GMT+4)**.
- Submissions after the deadline are not accepted.

## Language

**English only.** Your brief, commit messages and form answers must all be in English.
Clear, simple English is enough. We do not score grammar or style.

## Use of AI tools

You may use AI tools (for example ChatGPT) to help you understand topics.
However:

- Every resource you list must be a page you opened and read yourself.
- AI tools often give outdated or wrong facts. Check every fact against your resource.
- Do not list an AI chatbot as a resource.
- Be ready to explain your work in your own words in a later interview.

## Experience

You do not need previous GitHub experience. [GITHUB_HELP.md](GITHUB_HELP.md)
explains every step, both in the browser and with Git on your computer.

## Your privacy

- Do not put your phone number, national ID or home address in your repository.
- Your GitHub username will be visible in your public repository.
- We recommend hiding your email in commits (see GITHUB_HELP.md, Part 1).

## Resubmission

You may submit the form more than once before the deadline.
We use your **last** submission received before the deadline.

## Fair process

- Completing the mission does not guarantee selection.
- Every candidate is checked with the same rules.
- If you face a technical or access problem, contact **[support email]**
  **before** the deadline and explain what happened and what you tried.
