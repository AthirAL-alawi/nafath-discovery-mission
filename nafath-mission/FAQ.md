# Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I write in Arabic?**
No. All content must be in English: the brief, commit messages and form answers.
Simple, clear English is enough.

**Can I use ChatGPT or other AI tools?**
Yes, to help you understand. But every resource must be a real page you opened,
and every fact must match your resource. Do not list an AI tool as a resource.

**What counts as an "official or primary" resource?**
Standards and specifications (for example IETF RFCs, NIST publications, 3GPP),
laws and regulator websites, official documentation of a technology or vendor,
and published research papers. Blogs, Wikipedia and video tutorials may be used
for learning, but are weaker resources.

**Can I use more than one resource per field?**
No. List exactly **one** resource under each of the six fields.
Choose the best one.

**Which version should I complete?**
Only the version code in your invitation email (for example NAFATH-V2).

**Which track should I write in the "Track" line?**
The track you applied for: Software, AI, Mobile, Networking, Cybersecurity or Data.

**I made a mistake in a commit. Can I delete it?**
No. Do not rewrite history. Make an extra commit with a message starting with
`fix:`. Extra fix commits are allowed.

**Can I make the repository private?**
No. It must be public so we can review it. Do not put personal data in it.

**Can I submit the form again?**
Yes, before the deadline. We use your last submission.

**My push fails on macOS or Linux.**
GitHub needs a personal access token instead of your password.
See GITHUB_HELP.md, section B5. You can also switch to Option A (browser).

**Do I get points for finishing early or making extra commits?**
No. We do not reward speed or number of commits.

**Is there a minimum word count?**
Yes. Each section has a minimum and maximum. See the writing rules in your MISSION.md.

**Who do I contact?**
[support email]. Contact us before the deadline and describe what you tried.
We will not give answers to the mission.
